# 18. Future Scope

Future work may include:
- Carefully evaluated OCR for screenshot evidence
- QR image decoding with user-reviewed extraction
- Better multilingual/Hinglish evaluation
- Larger synthetic and consent-based datasets
- Temporal graph learning experiments
- Human evaluation of warning comprehension
- Safe, opt-in external reputation integrations
- Improved calibration and robustness testing
- Accessibility and usability improvements

Future scope must not be represented as implemented functionality. Real UPI/bank integration, transaction blocking, or payment reversal is outside the student prototype scope and would require authorized partnerships, security review, regulatory analysis, and production-grade safeguards.
